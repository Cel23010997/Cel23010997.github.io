BUSINESS COMMUNICATION / INDIVIDUAL COURSEWORK / 2021

ARMY Bomb Ver. 3

Turning sourced product footage into a clear promotional story, with an accompanying social-media graphic.

Adobe Premiere Pro2 promotional deliverablesIndividual academic project

My contribution

For an individual Business Communication Essentials assessment, I edited sourced product footage in Adobe Premiere Pro and designed an Instagram-style promotional graphic. My work covered clip selection, sequencing, timing, captions, humorous inserts and pacing.

The brief and constraint

The assignment called for a 2–3-minute persuasive presentation and a 50–100-word promotional message. These were the brief’s requirements. Footage from a fan-created unboxing video and official promotional material needed to feel like one coherent presentation.

How I shaped the story

I selected and arranged clips to introduce the product, adjusted their timing, and used captions and image inserts to support the presentation’s rhythm. The accompanying graphic translated the same promotional idea into a social-media format.

What I delivered

An edited promotional video and an Instagram-style graphic for the academic submission. This project shows my editing and visual-communication process; it was not a commercial campaign, and no sales, reach or engagement outcome is claimed.

What I would test next

I would ask a small group from the intended audience to explain the product’s key message after watching, then use their responses to refine the captions and pacing. This is a proposed next step, not testing completed for the assignment.
