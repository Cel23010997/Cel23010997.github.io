A little note on privacy

This portfolio shares selected professional and creative work. The contact section publishes my chosen professional email, celestetongwk@gmail.com. Public general resumes omit personal contact details. Home addresses, phone numbers, student identifiers and private coursework remain excluded. The concert photo is my own; identifying photo metadata has been removed. The Korean qualification is stated without displaying a certificate or its identifier.

Browsing the site

The portfolio has no analytics, advertising or contact form. Fonts and artwork are served with the site. Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages, whose service may process normal request information.

Playing the projects

Games load when you choose to play them. GuildUp stores demo tasks and sample guild data in your browser’s local storage; use its Reset demo button to clear that data. It does not send tasks or email to the original project services. It loads some third-party libraries and a font from public content-delivery services.

Order N Chaos opens the team’s external game site in a new tab. That site and external GitHub/LinkedIn links operate under their own policies.

The Concert Chaos ZIP contains the Windows runtime and supporting assets. Private coursework documents, source code, signing keys and debug symbols are excluded.
